This included building a wooden flap assembly from laser-cut components, precision-sanding the wooden guide channels so the flap and slat rods could slide smoothly without play, cutting metal guide rods to length using a Dremel, operating the laser cutter, and applying Monokote heat-shrink covering to finished surfaces."
